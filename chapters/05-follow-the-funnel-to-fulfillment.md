# Follow the funnel to fulfillment

Window A logged 16 offer-page visits and four fulfilled loans. Window B logged 12 visits and five fulfilled loans. A wins the traffic contest. B wins the handoff count. Neither result tells you that its channel caused the difference.

You will build a funnel with explicit denominators, compare a limited repeat-use measure and make an evidence-based revision rather than choosing a winner by whichever number looks highest.

## Name the denominator at every step

The [fictional funnel](../data/pilot-windows.json) counts unique eligible contacts, then unique visits, requests and handoffs attributed to each window. Assume requests in the fixture follow a visit and no contact belongs to both rows; real attribution and privacy rules require separate measurement design. Each window had its own eight-slot capacity.

| Stage | Window A | Window B |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Eligible contacts | 40 | 40 |
| Visits | 16 | 12 |
| Requests | 6 | 5 |
| Fulfilled loans | 4 | 5 |
| Visits per contact | 40% | 30% |
| Requests per visit | 37.5% | 41.7% |
| Fulfillments per request | 66.7% | 100% |
| Fulfillments per contact | 10% | 12.5% |

The difference in fulfillments per contact is 2.5 percentage points, or one extra handoff per 40 in these records. “100%” is five out of five, not evidence about all future requests. Before increasing reach, look into the two requests not fulfilled in A. They may reflect timing, form friction, cancellation or available kit fit. The fixture does not identify why.

For a limited repeat-use measure, only borrowers actually offered a comparable later window belong in the opportunity denominator. In the supplied example, two of three eligible prior borrowers from A borrowed again, and two of four from B did. These seven opportunity records are tiny and incomplete. They do not show that anyone found the service good or that a channel created loyalty. Record repeat behavior and missing opportunity separately.

## Set a revision rule

Suppose written route directions were inconsistent with pickup hours. Correct that observable mismatch before buying more traffic. If contact permission is unclear, pause instead of broadening the list. If all eight slots are full, add a waitlist measure rather than assuming the observed total bounds demand. A strong revision names one suspected obstacle, a checked change and a result that would make the team undo or revise it.

## Try it

Keep A's 40 contacts and 16 visits fixed. If one additional A request is fulfilled, its primary rate becomes 5/40 = 12.5%. The traffic rate is unchanged. Which stage improved mechanically? What evidence would you need to explain why?

**Check.** Fulfillment conditional on request rises from 4/6 to 5/6. Explain it only after checking the actual pickup record and whether staffing, kit fit, route or eligibility changed. Then build your own decision in [the marketing plan brief](../practice/pilot-brief.md).
